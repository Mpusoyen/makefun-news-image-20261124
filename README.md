A complete rebuild, "The Tuesday Record": a burgundy one-page apparatus that sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in 33 stories across 9 regions. An oxblood masthead with a gold counts strip carries the day's date in giant serif over an italic dek naming the three biggest stories (the markets surge, the Russian drone into Poland, the climate fight moving to Addis Ababa). Below sit a "Day in Numbers" strip and a region tally, then a new UTC hour-by-hour timeline with seven stops from the overnight Zaporizhzhia strike to the 21:00 Nasdaq close. The lead card gives Tuesday's market surge the biggest scene on the page — skyline, rising chart, candlesticks and a "+6.51%" ticker band — and all 13 pictured stories now carry full multi-element scenes drawn in code (a quadcopter over patchwork fields, a domed EU building under twelve stars with the "22" sanctions scroll, a sinking corvette, a Tiangong station with an approaching capsule, and more), each numbered, with a verification chip (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM), a named source, and a 2–3 line summary. A dot-matrix "Where It Happened" world map pins stories 1–13 with larger pins plus a Europe zoom inset for the crowded pins 2, 3 and 4, an "Also on Tuesday" rail carries stories 14–33 with a field tag and a named source on every item, and a burgundy Date Check footer states the one-day rule and lists what was deliberately left out with its real date (the Huang reinstatement, Mon 23 Nov; the COP31 collapse, Sun 22 Nov). Every story happened or was first reported that day. The image is drawn from scratch by draw.py (Python + Pillow, deterministic, no network) and shipped as entry.png; main.ts copies it to the output.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
